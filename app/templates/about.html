EasyShare (ShareShelf AI) is an intelligent, eco-friendly campus sharing and secondhand exchange platform created to help students, faculty, and local communities borrow, lend, and sell reusable items.

Our Mission

College life often requires items for brief intervals: an expensive graphing calculator during exam week, specific lab equipment for a single semester, or sports accessories on weekends. Instead of requiring students to purchase brand-new items that later sit idle in dorm rooms or end up in landfills, EasyShare bridges students with peers who already own these resources.

The TrustScore Philosophy

Campus sharing requires mutual confidence. EasyShare calculates an objective, transparent TrustScore based on:

  • Successful Handover & Completion (30%): Consistent track record of successful transactions.
  • Punctual Returns (30%): Returning borrowed items on or before the due date.
  • Community Reviews & Ratings (25%): Authentic feedback from verified exchange partners.
  • Adherence to Guidelines (15%): Minimal cancellations and zero reported honor-code violations.
Transparency Note: TrustScores are computed strictly on completed transactions and cannot be altered or fabricated. Reviews are accepted only once a QR-confirmed return or sale occurs.

Campus Honor Code

All members agree to maintain items in their original condition, communicate respectfully, show up on time for QR exchanges, and return items clean and undamaged.
