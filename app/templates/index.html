How {{ site_name }} Works

Three clear, secure steps to share resources across campus

1. Find or List Items

Search items to borrow for a few days or buy permanently. Listing an item takes under 60 seconds with category recommendations.

2. Secure Handover

Meet safely on campus. Verify item custody using cryptographic tokens and confirmed transaction records.

3. Return & Build Trust

Confirm returns on time. Rate your experience and build an audited TrustScore recognized by your campus peers.

Why EasyShare?

Designed Exclusively for Campus Needs

Students regularly spend hundreds of rupees or dollars each semester on single-use items: semester books, lab goggles, drafting kits, calculators, and cables. EasyShare provides a dedicated space to borrow or buy secondhand with verified peers.

Smart Availability Calendar

Atomic date booking ensures zero double-bookings or conflicting sale reservations.

Single-Use Handover Verification

Hashed verification tokens prevent fraud and guarantee proof of custody on return.
